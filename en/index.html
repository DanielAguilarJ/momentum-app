Frequently asked questions

Is Momentum free?

Yes. All features are available without payment and the app shows no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use Momentum without creating an account.

Can I block only Reels without blocking all of Instagram?

Yes. Momentum can block just the Reels section of Instagram or YouTube Shorts and let you keep using messages and the rest of the app. It also has a communication-only mode that allows messages and blocks feeds, stories and reels.

Why does it ask for the accessibility permission?

Android only lets an app see which app or section is open through the accessibility service. Momentum uses it only to apply your rules: when one matches, it shows the block screen or returns to the home screen. The service only turns on if you accept the notice in the app and enable it in Android settings.

What if I want to remove the block in a moment of temptation?

You can protect your rules with a password and use an emergency unlock. There is also nuclear mode, the strongest block: it locks the apps you choose for 1 to 3 months, and turning it off early means keeping Momentum open and visible for a long, uninterrupted wait (30 minutes by default).

Does it still work if Instagram or YouTube change their design?

Apps change their design often. If Instagram or YouTube change how a section is built, detection can fail until Momentum is updated. That is why it is worth keeping the app up to date.

Is Momentum an accessibility tool for people with disabilities?

No. It uses Android's accessibility service only for its blocking features.

Is it affiliated with Instagram, YouTube or TikTok?

No. Momentum is independent and has no relationship with those apps.
