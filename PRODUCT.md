# Product

## Platform

web (marketing site and guides for an Android app)

## Stack

The user asked for Astro and Three.js (2026-11-25: «animaciones 3D, Three.js, Astro, entre otras cosas… utiliza las mejores tecnologías»). The landing pages (`/` and `/en/`) are built with Astro in `_web/`; the guides stay generated by `_src/build.py`. Hosting is GitHub Pages, legacy mode, from the root of `main` in `DanielAguilarJ/momentum-app`, so built files are committed.

## Users

People, mostly Spanish-speaking in Latin America and secondarily English-speaking, who lose time to short-video feeds (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok) and social apps on Android and want a barrier that does not depend on willpower in the moment. Students and people who study or work on their phone are a confirmed audience of the guides.

## Product Purpose

Momentum is an Android app that blocks distracting apps and, more precisely, only the addictive section of an app (Reels inside Instagram, Shorts inside YouTube) while the rest of the app keeps working. Success for this site: visitors understand that in seconds, trust the permission it asks for, and install from Google Play.

## Positioning

Blocks the section, not the whole app: Reels and Shorts go away while Instagram messages keep working. Free, no ads, no account. Pairs blocking with a "life in weeks" view (an 80-year life as ~4,160 weeks) that reminds the user what the blocked time is for.

## Operating Context

The app is installed from Google Play (package `com.momentummm.app`). The user enables Android's accessibility service after an in-app disclosure («Aceptar y continuar» / «No, gracias»). Rules are set in Ajustes > Bloqueo dentro de Apps. Data stays on the phone.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed in code (2026-11-24/25):
- In-app section blocking with ready rules: Instagram Reels and Explorar, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels, and rules for TikTok, Snapchat and X.
- Daily time limits per app; schedules by day and hour; blocking by place or Wi-Fi network.
- Focus mode / Pomodoro (25/5 or custom), goals, streaks and grace days.
- Communication-only mode (messages allowed; feeds, stories and reels blocked).
- Password protection of rules and emergency unlock.
- Nuclear mode: blocks chosen apps for 1 to 3 months; removing it early needs Momentum open and visible for a long uninterrupted wait (30 minutes by default). It CAN be removed; never claim otherwise.
- Minimalist launcher (phone, messages, contacts).
- "My life in weeks" grid with widgets and wallpaper.
- Android 8.0+. Free, no ads, no account. Languages: es, en, pt, de, fr.
- Honest limitation: app redesigns by Instagram/YouTube can break detection until Momentum updates.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Momentum. Icon: glossy violet "M" with a clock on a deep indigo-violet tile (`assets/icon.png`). App palette (shared/…/ui/theme/Color.kt): Indigo 600 #4F46E5, Violet 500 #8B5CF6, Mint 500 #10B981, Amber 500 #F59E0B, Rose 500 #F43F5E. Not affiliated with Instagram, YouTube, TikTok; that disclaimer must stay on every page.

## Evidence on Hand

- Play Console 2026-11-24: 2 active installs, 10 listing visitors in 28 days, no reviews. There are NO testimonials, ratings, user counts, press or awards. Never invent them.
- Store screenshots are outdated (iPhone frames, English captions, a removed paid button); do not reuse them.
- Real copy for both languages lives in `_src/content_es_a.py` and `_src/content_en.py`.

## Product Principles

- Say exactly what the app does; every feature named must exist in code.
- The permission is the main trust hurdle; explain it plainly and early.
- The enemy is the infinite feed's design, not the user's weakness.
- Free, no ads, no account is a real advantage; say it without hype.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (3D and scroll animation must have a still fallback). Content must be readable without WebGL or JavaScript.
